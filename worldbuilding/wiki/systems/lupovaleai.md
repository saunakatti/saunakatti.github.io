# Lupovalea'i

A hidden system in [Navigator's Nebula](../regions/navigators_nebula.md).
The system is an important meeting point for people of
[Capricorn's Great Union](../factions/capricorns_great_union.md),
and the best known way of travelling to the union's main regions in
[The Val Conjunct](../regions/val_conjunct.md), using [Pol Atal](../other/pol_atal.md).

Precence of [Äen'e](../universe/aene.md) is high in the systems and in the nebula
surrounding it. Due to high cosmic activity in the nebula, travel to the system
through it is extremely difficult. The most used route is
[The Lumonea Gateway](../other/lumonea_gateway.md), largely used and protected by
[Olia's Toopas](../factions/olias_toopas.md).

Entry to planets of the Lupovalea'i with private starships is highly controlled
and usually ships stay docked on [The Lupovalea'i Star Haven](../settlements/lupovaleai_star_haven.md),
one of the largest known space station complexes. From there, you can take a transport
ship to the system capital, [Lupotopia](../settlements/lupotopia.md) on the planet
Lupovalea'i, or to some of the other planets. Usage of high power vehicles is
prohibited on planet surface.
