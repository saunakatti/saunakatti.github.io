# Tophu System

Tophu system is a system in [Atal'val](../regions/atalval.md).
It's the largest population centre of Atal'val and acts as its capital system. The nearby systems hold other important worlds of Atal'val. Tophu is also the administrative capital of [Capricorn's Great Union](../factions/capricorns_great_union.md).

## Planets

The Tophu System has 6 planets.

### Tophu
  - The largest ecumenopolis of Atal'val and the [Val Conjunct](../regions/val_conjunct.md) as a whole.
