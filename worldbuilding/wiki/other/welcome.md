# Welcome

Welcome to the new wiki!

Hi! This is a passion project of mine and not meant to be anything to take seriously.
It's around 10 years old and it has changed a lot since its beginning.

## Revamp

The project has been recently rewamped, the website being a newer part of that revamp.
The changes are not that radical but it's useful to know that things have changed a
little bit. The project will be carrying the name _"Ääri Project"_ instead of _"Galaksit_
_(Galaxies)"_ for now at least. The main change to the content is a rescaling of the environment. Instead of the project consisting of a galaxy cluster everything will be inside one galaxy.

### Why change it?

Main goal is to achive more connected lore, not to just endlessly add stuff with
no meaning. The sheer amount of somewhat useless content was getting a bit overwhelming. Some of the old wiki will be transferred here, some things will be rewritten.
