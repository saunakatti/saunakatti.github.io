# Capricorn's Great Nebula

---

The largest nebula of [Capricorn's Galaxy](./capricorns_galaxy.md), found in
the centre of the galaxy. Travel to the nebula is extremely dangerous due to
its high activity, making it a perfect hiding spot for regions
like the [Val Conjunct](./val_conjunct.md) or systems like [Ajankajo](../systems/ajankajo.md).
