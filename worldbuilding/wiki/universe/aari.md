# Ääri

Ääri, which could mean something like "The Endless" in this context, can be seen
as the stuff behind basically everything that exists. It is theorised to have a
physical form somewhere not accessible to anything else than Ääri itself. It
can also be argued that since Ääri probably makes up the dimensions, this "somewhere"
has to be something outside the dimensions. The physical manifestation of Ääri in
the dimensions is the existense of [Äen'e](./aene.md),
which are deeply connected to Ääri. Ääri has no beginning nor end.

## Knowledge on Ääri

Ääri as a concept is mainly unknown but many species have learned to manipulate
Ääri in small amounts through the use of [Äen'e](./aene.md). The groups that posess
greater knowledge on Ääri are mainly unknown and prefer to stay hidden or silent. A good
example of this would be the [Tellusians](../beings/tellusians.md) or beings found near
the [Foundries](./foundries.md). Smaller civilications are usually found in hidden regions
near [Emitters]().
