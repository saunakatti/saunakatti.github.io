Encyclopedia Usage Guide

  • Map Navigation: Click on any region or object on the map to open a short summary.
  • Directory: Use the "Directory" tab to browse and search all entries, also ones not on the map like Factions, Events and Beings.
  • Full Entries: Click "Read " to read in-depth entries linked to eachother.
  • Updates: See "Updates" to learn about changes and additions. You can also follow the update feed.
  • Settings: Change the visiblity of some elements on the map.
